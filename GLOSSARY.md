# Touch Typing Tutor

A browser app for practising touch typing: the learner retypes a Lesson while a virtual keyboard shows which key comes next.

## Language

**Lesson**:
One piece of practice text the learner retypes, offered in one of three Lesson lengths.
_Avoid_: Exercise, text, prompt

**Lesson length**:
How long a Lesson is: short, medium or long.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, level

**Typing Session**:
One attempt at a Lesson, from the first keystroke until the Lesson is completed or restarted. A Typing Session decides which characters are correct, what comes next, when the Lesson is complete, and the WPM and accuracy for that attempt.
_Avoid_: Attempt, run, game
